# Functional Requirements – Medical Practice CRM

## User Management
- The system shall allow administrators to manage staff accounts.
- The system shall support user authentication and secure login functionality.
- The system shall assign user roles and permissions.

## Patient Management
- The system shall allow users to add, update, and remove patient records.
- The system shall store patient contact and medical information.
- The system shall provide patient search functionality.

## Appointment Scheduling
- The system shall allow appointments to be created, edited, and cancelled.
- The system shall display appointment schedules in an organized format.
- The system shall notify users of scheduling conflicts.

## Clinical Workspace
- The system shall allow medical staff to access patient information and records.
- The system shall support viewing and updating consultation notes.

## Document Management
- The system shall allow secure uploading and storage of documents.
- The system shall allow users to view and download stored files.

## Dashboard and Reporting
- The system shall display system statistics and summaries.
- The system shall provide administrative overview dashboards.
- The system shall support filtering and searching of records.
